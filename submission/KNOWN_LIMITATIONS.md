# Known limits and unresolved gates

1. **No fresh confirmatory test.** GUIDE_Test and labels were previously accessed. Bootstrap intervals describe observed organizational variation but cannot provide a clean held-out claim.
2. **Field approval remains local-only.** Five descriptors are assumed usable for on-device exploration. The official point-in-time model-input allowlist is empty; feature timing and privacy approval remain unresolved. No data was sent to a hosted model in this work.
3. **Alert membership is ambiguous.** 70,702 composite alert keys have conflicting incident assignments. They are excluded from scoring, while every expert top-20 label stays in the denominator. This creates a structural recovery ceiling.
4. **The full-run candidate rule fails.** On the 499 ranked organizations, only 37,018/159,186,107 positive pairs are candidates (0.0233% recall). The 99% candidate-recall target is unmet, so downstream ranking cannot demonstrate the proposed end-to-end target. A [post-hoc 57-queue diagnostic](../outputs/recall_route_diagnostic_20261113_v1/aggregate.json) found that uncapped shared Category/technique/detector overlap still reached only 47.72% of positives at 59.45% pair coverage; it is not a held-out benchmark.
5. **The 95% end-to-end target is structurally blocked under current truth rules.** Only 9,125 of 9,980 full-run expert labels have unambiguous in-scope alert evidence, an upper bound of 91.43% even with perfect predictions. At least 356 additional labels would need a source-validated membership resolution to reach 95% without changing the denominator.
6. **The local Qwen test is narrow.** It uses two previously accessed small organizations and 15 score-stratified pairs per queue. All 30 selected pairs are negative, so positive-pair recall and F1 cannot be estimated. C produced only singleton groups; its 11/40 recovered labels are mostly a single-alert incident result, not evidence of model correlation skill. It batches model decisions, lacks a P/V revision loop, and has no independent citation review. B2/B3 have negative-pair diagnostics only; B4 and a fully matched C/P/V baseline remain unmeasured.
7. **Ranking semantics are provisional.** The exact active-queue universe and numeric rank direction in the supplied queue file are not independently settled. Rank-1-highest is the primary local assumption; reverse-direction sensitivity is retained where available.
8. **Baselines differ from the original plan.** Detector severity is unavailable, so the full B0-like ranker uses group alert count. The earlier CP/CPV results are statistical proxies. None should be labeled a complete LLM-agent comparison.
9. **Operational outcomes are unmeasured.** There is no analyst time study, blinded reviewer agreement, calibrated priority score, containment result, prospective latency/cost benchmark, or production SOC validation.
10. **Dashboard scope is illustrative.** The simulation plays fictional synthetic events and is separate from empirical GUIDE/Qwen metrics. Browser interaction QA could not be completed in the available browser environment; static build and source checks are recorded in [dashboard verification](DASHBOARD_VERIFICATION.md).

These limits prevent a claim of 95% recovery, confirmatory superiority, deployment readiness, or military certification.
