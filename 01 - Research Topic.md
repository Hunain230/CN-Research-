# Proposed Research Topic

## Working title

**Evidence-Grounded Multi-Agent Reasoning for Security Alert Triage**

## Current status

This is a working topic to be evaluated against the three selected papers. It is not treated as validated, novel, or sufficiently narrow in advance.

## Domain boundaries

- **Network intrusion detection:** detecting potentially malicious activity in network data.
- **Network traffic classification:** assigning traffic or flows to classes; this may support detection but is not itself SOC alert triage.
- **Security alerts:** signals produced by security controls or analytics that require interpretation.
- **SOC alert triage:** prioritizing, contextualizing, grouping, and deciding the next investigative action for alerts.
- **Incident investigation:** collecting and reasoning over evidence to determine scope, cause, entities, and chronology.
- **Incident response:** taking containment, eradication, recovery, or other response actions.
- **Cyber threat intelligence (CTI):** knowledge about threats, actors, tactics, techniques, indicators, and relationships.
- **APT profiling:** constructing a structured profile of an advanced persistent threat actor or campaign from CTI evidence.

These activities overlap, but they are not interchangeable. Evidence from detection, simulation/response, or CTI profiling supports the proposed topic only to the extent that its mechanisms transfer to alert-triage decisions.

## Evidence categories used in this vault

### AUTHOR CLAIM

Content explicitly stated, reported, or demonstrated in a paper.

### OUR OBSERVATION

Critical interpretation made during this review. It must not be attributed to the paper's authors.

### RESEARCH OPPORTUNITY

A possible direction derived from cross-paper synthesis. It is not a claim of novelty in the wider literature.
