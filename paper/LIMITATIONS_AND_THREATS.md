# Limitations and threats to validity

1. **Synthetic external validity.** Phase 33's true source roots, causal IDs, claim corruption and availability times were generated. The result cannot establish independence of physical sensors, resistance to coordinated forgery, or real SOC performance.
2. **One frozen holdout.** The candidate was evaluated once on 15 holdout queues after development. The paired +10 incident gain is observed, without a new confidence interval or significance claim for that holdout.
3. **Simulator-family dependence.** Train, development and holdout use separated seeds and template variants within a controlled generator family. This is stronger than fitting and testing on the same queue but weaker than transfer to an unrelated organization.
4. **Metric construct.** The original evaluator requires 80% incident completeness and 80% group purity, 20 target incidents per queue, a top-20 rank cutoff and 100-alert group cap. Other SOC workflows may value different errors and queue definitions.
5. **False-merge risk.** Zero false pairs and mixed groups were observed on Phase 33; this is not a universal guarantee. Phase 25 and Phase 28 found unsafe unions despite strong aggregate metrics. Queue-level audits are necessary.
6. **Missing evidence and safe abstention.** Rejected genuine claims (35 in Phase 33's post-truth audit) mean the verifier sacrifices some recall. A single global abstention rate is not meaningful from the stored decision categories without a frozen common denominator.
7. **Development selection.** Phase 32's +1 candidate was chosen after development experiments and missed its own 85% development gate. Phase 33 subsequently froze and tested it under a new explicit holdout instruction. The Phase 33 result does not make Phase 32's decision retroactively positive.
8. **No direct cross-phase ranking.** Phase 16, 25--30, 31--33 and 34--38 populations differ. Percentages across them are descriptive history, not paired effect estimates.
9. **Real data blocked.** GUIDE's local export lacks original observation provenance and evidence-availability timing; 70,702 composite alert keys have conflicting incident assignments. Queue judgments and approved fields are unresolved. Phase 39 made no predictions.
10. **Operational outcomes unmeasured.** No analyst study, shadow deployment, response action, real latency, throughput, calibration or cost-benefit measurement was performed.
