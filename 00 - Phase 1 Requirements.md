# Phase 1 - Literature Review and Research Gap Identification

## Exact assignment purpose

> The purpose of this phase is to develop the ability to critically read and analyze recent research literature related to your proposed research topic. You need to identify established methodologies, datasets, models, evaluation strategies, reported findings, limitations, and potential research gaps.

Source: [Phase 1 assignment, p. 1]

## Paper-selection requirements

Each group must identify and critically study the selected papers. Each paper must:

- be published in 2026;
- be a research article, not merely an editorial, opinion article, book chapter, conference paper, news article, or general webpage;
- be published in a Q1-ranked journal;
- be directly relevant to the student's proposed research topic;
- present an identifiable research methodology, experimental setup, or analytical framework;
- include sufficient information about the dataset/data source, methods, and evaluation to allow critical analysis; and
- preferably be from a reputable publisher/journal such as Springer Nature, Elsevier, IEEE, Wiley, ACM, Taylor & Francis, or another recognized academic publisher.

Source: [Phase 1 assignment, p. 1]

## Number of papers

Exactly **three (3)** papers must be identified and critically studied. [Phase 1 assignment, p. 1]

## Publication-year requirement

All three papers must be published in **2026**. [Phase 1 assignment, pp. 1-2]

## Q1 requirement

All three papers must be **Q1 journal research papers** and each selected paper must be published in a **Q1-ranked journal**. [Phase 1 assignment, p. 1]

## Required critical-summary components

The required deliverable is a **one-page critical summary** covering:

- limitations explicitly mentioned by the authors;
- limitations identified by the students;
- dataset limitations;
- methodological limitations;
- evaluation limitations;
- generalizability issues;
- missing experiments; and
- potential directions for future research.

Source: [Phase 1 assignment, pp. 1-2]

## Required comparison-table columns

The cross-paper comparison table must use these columns, in this order:

| # | Title | Year of Publication | Dataset Used | Preprocessing Techniques | Models / Techniques Used | Evaluation Metrics Used | Results | Limitations / Research Gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

The table must include one row for each of the three papers. It **must not simply copy sentences from the papers**; students must synthesize the information and make the differences among the three studies clear. [Phase 1 assignment, p. 2]

## Research-gap requirements

Based on analysis of the three papers, students must identify **at least three potential research gaps**. The required synthesis table has these columns:

| Research Gap | Evidence from Paper 1 | Evidence from Paper 2 | Evidence from Paper 3 | Proposed Research Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Source: [Phase 1 assignment, p. 2]

## Formatting and submission requirements

- Each critical summary must be one page. [Phase 1 assignment, pp. 1-2]
- The comparison and research-gap tables must use the specified column structures. [Phase 1 assignment, p. 2]
- The assignment PDF does **not clearly specify** a citation style, font, spacing, margins, combined-document page limit, file format, filename convention, cover page, submission platform, or deadline.
